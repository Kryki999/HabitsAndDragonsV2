> Torrika **nie ma na tym kadrze**. ★1 **Closed Way** = osobny pin + osobne kadry ([`prompt-closed-way-closeup.md`](prompt-closed-way-closeup.md)) — **nie** ta brama.

- Camera: standing in a forest glade, looking toward a cottage and a darker path. Vertical depth.
- Bottom ~10%: empty moss / path only.

HOTSPOTS (two, readable in 0.5s):
1) A forest cottage grown INTO the trees — organic, mossy, mushroomy, living-wood hermit hut (Radagast-cottage VIBE only: do NOT copy Lord of the Rings, do NOT draw Radagast). It is a DWARF SMITHY: anvil, forge chimney, COLD hearth (no roaring fire), a couple of unfinished sword-shapes. Tappable house. Nobody standing in front of it. The smith is inside — do not draw the dwarf.
2) Deeper in: a RUINED mossy gate / crooked timber arch, broken and overgrown — NOT a neat park gate, NOT ordinary city doors. Behind it the forest turns worse: clawed/scratched tree trunks, darker canopy, a path you can walk. Tappable. This is the basilisk way. No full monster in frame.

Foreground: one unused hammer or bent blade as set dressing.
Background: the woods get meaner with distance. No palace, no market, no pirate cove, no bandit camp, no wizard.

Characters: NONE. Empty location. No dwarf, no basilisk body, no crowd.

Lighting: daylight on the cottage side; cooler/darker toward the ruined gate. Not night, not full grimdark, not a horror poster.

Ban: Torrik/dwarf in shot, Radagast, Gandalf, LotR still, Disney cottage, neat fence gate, city square, roaring forge, giant basilisk, gore, petrified people, readable text, UI.
