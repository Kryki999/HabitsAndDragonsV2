## SCENE L2 — Skarne (wklej pod STYLE LOCK) — **generuj to najpierw**

```text
SCENE:
- Format: vertical 9:16 mobile RPG BOSS encounter still (Champion fight screen). New drawing. No UI, no HUD, no readable letters.
- Camera: deeper on the Closed Way, at a false guide-post / last trail halt. We are in his hunting ground now, not at the crown barricade, not in a wizard tower, not in the smith cottage. Vertical depth.
- Bottom ~10%: empty roots / packed earth only (fight UI).
- Hotspot that must read in 0.5s: SKARNE the mage-hunter WAITING for the lost — a living human guide-hunter, calm, in control. The weapon is a BOW-STAFF hybrid plus a controlled GREEN plant aura. Not a roar. Not a photo pose.

BOSS:
- Living human man, weathered forest guide, compact readable cartoon. NOT an elf, NOT Legolas, NOT a WoW night elf, NOT an Ent, NOT Groot, NOT Poison Ivy, NOT a wolfman, NOT a bigger Rook, NOT the tavern wizard copied from the ref.
- Hunter FIRST: cloak, leather, moss stains, a guide's hood or cap — not a full wizard robe, not a spellbook.
- Hero weapon: a BOW-STAFF — one object that reads as both a wooden walking-staff AND a bow (grown wood arc, vine as string, leafy nocks). Planted or held like a guide's staff that can shoot. Chunky game silhouette, readable in thumbnail.
- Color: forest greens + a controlled SAGE/LEAF-GREEN aura (cel-shade glow on the bow-staff and a few vines). NOT neon. NOT teal (Marrow). NOT red (Rook). NOT violet (wizard).
- Seat: he sits or leans on a thick ROOT / the last trail-post, waiting for travelers — rank = this path is his. Ban: standing Legolas power-pose, heroic flex, mid-leap shot.
- Face: the helpful-guide smile gone wrong. Contempt: you are lost, he will "help." Alive, mean toward the visitor. NOT sad, NOT wizard deadpan, NOT Disney cute ranger.

SET:
- Foreground: one living vine-snare as a simple loop, one snuffed lantern — chunky props.
- Mid: looping woods, false trail markers, plants that lean toward him (he can nudge the forest). 1–2 trees with cartoon faces grown into bark (moss, simple, "settled"/cared-for — NOT screaming, NOT corpses, NOT a statue garden). Wood curse, NOT stone petrify.
- Background: darker canopy, simplified. Optional tiny crown-seal shape on a broken post (no letters). No cottage, no city, no ocean, no pirate cave, no temple interior yet (that is ★2).
- Extra characters: none as a crew. The trees are the victims. No wolves stealing the shot.

Lighting: cooler forest daylight + small controlled green aura. Still day. Not night-noir, not grimdark, not tavern candles.

Ban: wizard copied from the ref, Torrik, Stillgaze basilisk, Gutterjack, Rook, Marrow, pirates, goblins, Legolas, night elf, Radagast, Gandalf, Groot, fireballs, spellbook, teal glow, red arrow, gore, corpse pile, readable text, UI, player character.
```

## SCENE L1 — Thornpack (druga generacja)

```text
SCENE:
- Format: vertical 9:16 mobile RPG DUNGEON encounter still (Elite fight screen). New drawing. No UI, no HUD, no readable letters.
- Camera: just PAST the crown barricade, on the looping path. We entered the Closed Way. Not Skarne's halt yet. Vertical depth.
- Bottom ~10%: empty earth / moss only (fight UI).
- Hotspot that must read in 0.5s: the THORNPACK — 3–5 cursed forest beasts (wolves / boars / hounds as chunky cartoon, mossy, thorn-collars or vine-snares on them) DRIVING prey toward a tree-snare. Functioning pack vs the visitor. NOT a group photo. NOT cute wolves. NOT about to fight each other.
- Foreground: one vine-loop snare, one bone-simple shape — no gore pile.
- Mid: denser woods, one cartoon bark-face tree (small). Daylight through leaves.
- Background: the path going deeper (promise of the guide). No Skarne in frame. No cottage. No city.
- Characters: pack only. Alive-cursed, mean toward outsiders. NOT skeletons, NOT goblins, NOT a dragon.
- Lighting: cooler daylight. Not night. Not Skarne's green aura stealing the shot (maybe a faint green tint in the vines only).
- Ban: Skarne, wizard, Torrik, basilisk, pirates, bandits, Legolas, gore, cute Disney animals, lineup, readable text, UI, player character.
```
