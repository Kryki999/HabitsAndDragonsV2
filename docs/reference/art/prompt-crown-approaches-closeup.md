# Prompt — Crown Approaches close-up (przedpola / rozbójnicy)

> **Kadr 2** lokacji R1 S. Dwa hotspoty: strażnica (Pell) + obóz Inkless (loch). Miasto w tle jak pałac na rynku — obietnica, nie tap.  
> Kit: [`style-kit.md`](style-kit.md) — załącz **tylko maga**, STYLE LOCK + SCENE.  
> Produkt: [`23`](../../23-act1-location-brainstorm.md) §D2.

**Nie** dokładaj stolicy ani zatoki. Miasto opisz w SCENE jako małe tło. Inkless = **żywi** hulacy, nie kości z Teeth.

## Layout (LOCK)

Jak hub: pionowa głębia, **dwa** czytelne tapnięcia, dół pusty pod taby.

```text
9:16

┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│  Crownhaven MALUTKIE na wzgórzu │  dachy terracotta + skrawek pałacu
│                                 │
│  [STRAŻNICA]     [OBÓZ w lesie] │  dwa hotspoty, podobna waga
│   Pell olewa      Inkless hulają│
│                                 │
│  mostek / droga / strumień teal │
│                                 │
│ BLEED: trawa / droga            │  dolne ~10%
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Hotspot | Co malujesz | Funkcja |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Strażnica + most** | Kamienna wieża, wyblakły sztandar-kształt (bez liter), mostek | Tap → Pell |
| **Obóz** | Namioty/płótna w zagajniku **tuż pod wieżą**, ognisko, łup | Tap → loch Inkless → Rook |
| **Tło** | Miasto daleko, uproszczone | Nie drugi Crownhaven-plac |

Pell = olewa, ale **ciepły slacker** (uśmieszek, wzruszenie), nie depresja. Inkless = hulanka **kosztem** drogi/wieży, między sobą — nie fotka z imprezy.

## SCENE — wklej pod STYLE LOCK

```text
SCENE:
- Format: vertical 9:16 mobile RPG location still. Crown Approaches — the threshold south of the capital. New drawing. No UI, no HUD, no readable letters.
- Camera: standing on the dirt road just outside the city, looking toward a watchtower and a copse. Vertical depth. Threshold, not suburbs, not a city square.
- Bottom ~10%: empty dirt road / simple grass only.
- Hotspots that must read in 0.5s (TWO, similar weight, like stall vs tavern):
  1) LEFT: a stone watchtower and a small bridge over a teal cartoon stream. Tappable building.
  2) RIGHT: a bandit camp in the trees RIGHT UNDER THE TOWER'S NOSE — tents, a campfire, loot sacks. Tappable camp. The joke is the guards can see them and do nothing.
- Background/upper: Crownhaven SMALL on the hill — a few terracotta roofs and a tiny cream palace, simplified, low detail. Same kingdom, not a second market plaza. No fountain, no vegetable stall, no tavern filling the frame.
- Foreground: one milestone stone, maybe a faded crown-banner shape (no letters) — set dressing.
- Characters:
  - At the tower: SERGEANT PELL, readable midground NPC. Lazy but WARM-HUMAN, not depressed: wry smirk, slouched, spear unused, maybe a small wave or a shrug as if to say "yeah I know." He is the player's slacker, not a sad extra. Graphic eyes, not Disney. He is not heroic armor-ad.
  - At the camp: 3–4 LIVING human bandits, SMALLER than the tower. United crew dividing loot, laughing AT a spilled merchant bag. NOT skeletons, NOT goblins, NOT mug-clinking buddies, NOT about to brawl. Greedy, alive, not sad.
  - Optional: 1 extra guard on the wall, tiny, also wry-lazy not sad.
- Lighting: outdoor daylight / golden hour. Campfire as a small orange shape. Not night, not grimdark forest, not fog covering the frame.
- Ban: wizard, tavern interior, market stall, vendor woman, pirate skeletons, goblin bandits, sad lifeless good NPCs, funeral, group-photo posing, gore, palace as the main subject, cobblestone city square, Jack Sparrow, readable text, UI.
```

- [ ] Pell = leniwy ale **ludzki** (uśmieszek), nie smutny extra
- [ ] Inkless hulają **między sobą**, nie pozują do fotki w kamerę
- [ ] Zero kości / goblinów / nocy
