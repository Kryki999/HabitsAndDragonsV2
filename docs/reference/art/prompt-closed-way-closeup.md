```text

- Format: vertical 9:16 mobile RPG location still. The Closed Way. New drawing. No UI, no HUD, no readable letters.
- Camera: standing on an abandoned forest road, looking INTO a sealed path. Vertical depth. This is a DIFFERENT woods-sector than the smith's mossy glade — no cottage, no anvil, no basilisk gate.

HOTSPOT (one, readable in 0.5s):
- The MOUTH of the Closed Way: the path dives into a throat of woven thorns and looping branches. Darker inside, daylight outside. Tappable. Bigger than any leftover crown prop. Not an X-board barricade filling the frame. Not a garden gate.

CROWN JOKE (side, losing):
- Official closure leftovers: broken posts, cracked wax-seal SHAPE (no letters), standing lanterns snuffed cold.

TELL (small, background — not the subject):
- 2–3 trees with cartoon faces grown into bark ("settled" people, maybe a hunter cap — not Treebeard, not screaming). One crooked guide-post pointing into the throat. Empty snares in the thorns. Optional tiny roadside niche with BLACK wax (★2 breadcrumb — not a chapel). Wood-and-moss curse, not stone petrify (Stillgaze).

Foreground: one dead lantern, one loop of unused rope / snare as a simple shape.
Background: denser canopy, false trail forks, empty animal dens. Cooler green daylight through leaves — still DAY, not night-noir, not a horror poster, not grimdark.

Characters: NONE. Empty location. No hunter, no wizard, no dwarf, no wolves in the foreground, no crowd.

Ban: Skarne, Torrik, cottage, mossy park-gate, basilisk, pirates, bandits, city, palace filling the frame, Legolas, Radagast, LotR still, gore, corpse pile, readable text, UI.
```
