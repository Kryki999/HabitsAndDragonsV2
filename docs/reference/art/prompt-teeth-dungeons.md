### Nowa SCENE — Marrow

```text
SCENE:
- Format: vertical 9:16 mobile RPG BOSS encounter still (Elite fight screen). New drawing. No UI, no HUD, no readable letters.
- Camera: deeper in the hideout — a drowned captain's cabin jammed into the cliff. No daylight. Not Rook's tent. Not a gym.

- Rank props: tricorn or captain's hat, ruined fancy coat, one bony hand resting on the chair (relaxed — the necklace does the work).
- Hero prop: Necklace of the Still Tide — chunky teal/cyan pendant on his chest, graphic glow, readable in thumbnail. No letters.
- Foreground: one grog barrel, one chest — simple shapes. He is not flexing.
- Mid: cramped cabin + cave rock, lanterns still burning, wet floor, a few coins.
- Background: dark wood and rock, simplified. Optional: 0–1 tiny cursed sailor in the corner, subordinate, not a drinking buddy. No beach, no palace, no red flying arrow (that is Rook).

- Lighting: amber lanterns + controlled teal glow from the necklace. Not HDR, not grimdark, not Rook's red ring.
- Ban: wizard, tavern, market, vendor, bigger-Rook, muscle captain, standing power-pose, goblins, gore, buddy toast, infighting, Jack Sparrow, Yondu, red arrow, readable text, UI, player character.
```
